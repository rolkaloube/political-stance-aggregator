Sample Harrisburg Local Election Candidate Guide

This page summarizes a sample of publicly available candidate information from recent Harrisburg local elections, mainly the 2025 Harrisburg mayoral race and selected 2025 Harrisburg City Council candidates. It is designed as a quick-reference file, not a comprehensive voter guide.

Compiled March 26, 2026 • Sample only • Public-source summary

How to use this file

Candidate pages and local coverage vary widely in detail. For that reason, this guide separates three kinds of information: office/background, campaign themes or priorities, and election outcome. Where detailed issue positions were not easy to verify from public sources, this guide says so instead of guessing.

Mayoral race sample

Mayor

Wanda Williams

Incumbent mayor of Harrisburg; won re-election in the November 4, 2025 general election.

  • Role/background: Ballotpedia lists Williams as the sitting mayor of Harrisburg.
  • Public priorities found in reporting: At her January 2026 swearing-in, Williams emphasized infrastructure, responsible growth, stability, long-term planning, homeownership, blight reduction, workforce development, and improved cooperation in city government.
  • Election result: Defeated Dan Miller in the 2025 general election.

Status: Won 2025 general election

Mayor

Dan Miller

Harrisburg city treasurer and former councilperson/controller; ran for mayor in 2025.

  • Role/background: Ballotpedia says Miller has served Harrisburg as councilperson, controller, and treasurer, and has a background as a CPA and business owner.
  • Campaign themes found in Ballotpedia survey: Miller described himself as the most qualified candidate based on financial, management, and city-government experience. He argued city management was inefficient and said he could improve operations.
  • Additional campaign framing: FOX43 reported that Miller entered the race pledging “fresh leadership.”
  • Election result: Lost to Wanda Williams in the 2025 general election after also losing the Democratic primary and advancing separately as the Republican write-in nominee.

Status: Candidate sample included for comparison

City Council sample

Public information for council candidates was more uneven than for the mayoral race. The entries below are a sample that combines Ballotpedia election records with local reporting where available.

City Council

Ausha Green

  • Role/background: Ballotpedia lists Green as an at-large Harrisburg City Council member first elected in 2018.
  • 2025 status: Re-elected in the November 4, 2025 general election.
  • Primary standing: One of the top four finishers in the 2025 Democratic primary.
  • Public issue detail: Publicly accessible summary material located here was limited; this sample focuses on office and election context.
City Council

Robert Lawson

  • Role/background: Ballotpedia lists Lawson as an at-large council member beginning January 5, 2026, and identifies him as a former council member.
  • 2025 status: Won one of four council seats in the November 4, 2025 general election.
  • Primary standing: Finished among the four successful Democratic primary candidates.
  • Public issue detail: Easily verifiable issue-specific platform detail was limited in the sources sampled for this file.
City Council

Elyse Irvis

  • Role/background: Part of the United Neighbors for Harrisburg slate announced in February 2025.
  • Shared themes from slate launch: TheBurg reported that Irvis, Andrea Grove, and Basir Vincent presented a shared vision centered on economic growth, affordable housing development, and community building.
  • 2025 status: Ran in the Democratic primary but did not advance to the winning four spots.
City Council

Basir Vincent

  • Role/background: Also part of the United Neighbors for Harrisburg slate.
  • Shared themes from slate launch: Economic growth, affordable housing development, and community building.
  • 2025 status: Ran in the Democratic primary but did not win one of the four nominations.
