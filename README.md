## **Prompt**
Provide a tool that can provide you with a list of upcoming local elections, who the candidates are, and summarize the political views of each candidate. 

## **Problem**
When election season comes up, it is important to plan to vote and know who your potential elected leaders are. Knowing the local candidates and what campaign issues they are running on is difficult and very time-consuming. 

## **MVP**
A location-based election information tool that helps voters easily understand what races are on their ballot and when those elections are taking place. By using a user’s location, the system consolidates all upcoming political races relevant to their area, reducing the need to search across multiple government websites. 

Key MVP Components: 

* Location-based detection of relevant political races 
* Centralized / aggregated, list of upcoming elections and key dates 
* Candidate listings for each race 

## **Beyond MVP**
* Provide a summary of each politician's campaign. 
* Provide a summary of how each politician voted and what bills they attempted to pass if they were an existing elected official. 
* Link to new articles that reference politicians. 
* Build a UI to take input data and display information.
